How it works

From trading fee to your wallet in four steps

The whole loop runs on-chain every round. Every swap and payout has a transaction hash you can check.

  1. Fees are collected

    Each buy and sell on the $AIDIV/$AI pair generates fees in both tokens. They go to the distributor wallet.

  2. Swapped to $AI

    The $AIDIV part is sold for $AI in small slices with a slippage cap, so the chart isn't dumped.

  3. Holders snapshotted

    Divi reads every Transfer event and skips LP pools, contracts and burn addresses.

  4. $AI airdropped

    90% is split pro rata by balance and sent in batched transactions. Tiny shares roll over until they're worth the gas.

Built to be trusted

A distributor you can audit

Never pays twice

Each payout is signed and recorded before it's sent. If the bot restarts mid-round, it resumes the exact same transactions.

On-chain proof

Every swap and airdrop links to the Robinhood Chain explorer. Totals are posted to Telegram after each round.

Fair by design

LP pools, the launchpad contract and team wallets are excluded, so dividends reach real holders only.

Gentle on the chart

Fee tokens are sold in capped slices with slippage protection instead of one big market sell.

Batched to save gas

Up to 100 holders are paid in a single transaction through a Disperse contract.

Small bags count

Shares too small to send are saved and paid out once they add up. Nothing is forfeited.

FAQ

Questions holders ask

Do I need to claim or stake?

No. Keep $AIDIV in your own wallet and $AI arrives automatically every round.

Why am I paid in $AI and not ETH?

$AIDIV trades against $AI, so fees already arrive partly in $AI. Paying in $AI keeps the loop simple and adds steady buy pressure on $AI.

My wallet received nothing this round. Why?

Very small shares are held until they pass the minimum payout, so gas doesn't eat your dividend. They are paid in a later round. Wallets that are smart contracts are excluded.

Where does the 10% operations share go?

To a public operations wallet that pays for bot gas, development, listings and marketing. Its address is published at launch.
